THE PROBLEM

Evidence shouldn't be
a separate project.

Checking URLs, tracing redirects, taking screenshots and assembling the results by hand scatters the context across different tools. The report becomes another task after the investigation.

VRIOSCU connects those jobs in one structured validation run. Technical metrics and captured pages stay associated with the endpoint they describe.
